# after-the-echo

> *Some things are removed but never truly gone. The past leaves traces for those willing to look.*

---

Every life has an opening and an ending. Recognition falls between them, but it is not another date of the same kind.

Not everything visible tells the whole story. Repositories remember what their surface no longer shows. Look deeper — the revisions hold a record that the present has discarded.

The old record is not the treasure. It is a signpost. Follow where it leads, and learn about the author it names.

Three milestones define the key: the **beginning**, the **recognition**, and the **end**. Each is a date. Preserve their chronological order. Where a full date exists, keep the year, the month, and the day. Where only a year is known, use the year alone. Separate each milestone with a hyphen. Format dates as numerals: `YYYYMMDD` or `YYYY`.
